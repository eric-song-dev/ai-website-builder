# Clear conversion pages

The hero should state who the offer is for, the concrete outcome, and the primary next action. Follow with three to five benefits, a simple process, credible evidence, common objections, and a repeated call to action. Specific copy outperforms vague superlatives.

Keep visual hierarchy strong: one dominant action per section, short paragraphs, meaningful labels, and proof close to the claim it supports. The final section should resolve uncertainty and make the next step low friction.
